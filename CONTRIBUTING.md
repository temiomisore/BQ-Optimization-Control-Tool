## Author Sign-Off (DCO)

All commits and contributions to this repository require an explicit **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)** sign-off (`git commit -s`), adding the following line to each commit message:

```text
Signed-off-by: Temitope Omisore <temiomisore@google.com>
```

By signing off, the author certifies that they have the right to submit the work under the **Apache License, Version 2.0** and that no proprietary customer confidential data or credentials are included in the repository.
